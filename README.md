**Developed by: Anthonia Othetheaso**

A comprehensive web-based application designed to combat food waste through intelligent inventory tracking, expiry monitoring, and community-driven food donation. FoodSave empowers users to reduce household food waste while contributing to **SDG 2: Zero Hunger** by facilitating food sharing within communities.

## 🌟 Overview

FoodSave goes beyond tracking food items — it creates a sustainable ecosystem for reducing waste and redistributing surplus food to communities in need.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Food Inventory Management
- Add and manage food items with expiry and category details
- Automatic expiry notifications
- Storage location tracking (pantry, fridge, freezer)

### ❤️ Community Donation System
- Create and share food donations in your community
- Claim food donations from others
- Real-time donation status updates

### 📈 Analytics & Insights
- Visual dashboards on waste reduction
- Track meals saved & environmental impact (CO₂ & water savings)

### 🔐 User Management
- Secure login & authentication
- Personalized dashboard and activity history

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

Backend: Flask, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login  
Frontend: Bootstrap 5, Jinja2, Chart.js  
Database: SQLite / PostgreSQL  
Deployment: Render
